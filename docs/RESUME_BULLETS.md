# Resume Bullets: Singapore Banking Loan Portfolio & Sector Exposure Analytics

- Cleaned and validated 62 months of official MAS/Singapore DOS commercial bank lending data (~S$966B portfolio, 1,674 observations) in Python and pandas, then used DuckDB SQL joins, CTEs, and window functions to quantify year-over-year loan growth, business sector concentration, and each sector's contribution to S$67.4B in net business lending growth.
- Built a star-schema Power BI data model (FactLoans, DimMonth, DimCategory) with DAX measures for dynamic YoY growth, top-3 sector concentration, and growth-contribution calculations, and designed a two-page interactive dashboard presenting 60.22% business-lending share and 76.87% top-3 sector concentration, with every figure cross-checked against independent SQL results.
